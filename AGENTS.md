# RepForge repository instructions

## Work-package effort and progress reporting

- Before starting or continuing work on any active work package, read `docs/governance/effort-points-and-progress-reporting.md`.
- Treat its effort-point allocation, stage-level breakdown, evidence-based earned-progress rules, and reporting fields as mandatory work-package requirements.
- Every active WP progress update must report allocated points, earned points, completion percentage, remaining points, estimate confidence, evidence baseline/date, blockers/dependencies, scope changes, and acceptance status.
- Do not infer progress from elapsed time, activity, assignment, or implementation claims without evidence. Do not report 100% before all in-scope acceptance criteria are met and the required owner acceptance is recorded.
- Keep the WP delivery plan/record current with stage or deliverable point allocations that sum exactly to the approved WP total.
- Use the approved baseline allocations in the governance standard. Any reallocation or change to total points requires a traceable owner decision; never silently change historical allocations.
- Programme roll-ups must calculate and report both full-programme and core-programme weighted completion, state the denominator and snapshot date, and link to the underlying WP evidence.
- Do not reopen or rewrite closed WP delivery records merely to retrofit progress reporting. Apply the reporting requirement to active WPs and new WPs; retain historical baseline estimates separately.
