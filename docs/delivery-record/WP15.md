## Stage 1 — entry-unit domain (implemented 2026-11-06)
- `app/lib/domain/units/entry_unit.dart`: `EntryUnit` enum (g, kg, mg, ml, l) with exact factor, reference basis, entry decimals (4 for kg and l, 1 for g and ml, 0 for mg), `toCanonical` (multiply, then the §2.1 store step through `Quantity.of`), `parse` (plain decimals), `fromCanonical` (exact division; refuses a cross-basis quantity), `forBase` (units offered for a food's basis; mg only on request), `canonicalFor`.
- No new conversion framework: every conversion ends in the existing `Quantity.of` and every nutrient calculation stays in `NutrientProfile.forQuantity`.
- Specification: revision **1.4 (proposed)** — §2.3 entry-unit rules; worked examples E42–E49. Revision 1.3 stays the approved text until the owner approves 1.4.
- Tests: `test/domain/entry_unit_test.dart` (9 tests), `test/domain/nutrition_spec_examples_units_test.dart` (E42–E49, 8 tests). Executed locally on Flutter 3.47.9: 17 passed. `flutter analyze --fatal-infos` clean on the new files. CI evidence: pending (first push of the branch with code).

### Decisions taken in stage 1 (recorded; owner may override)
- **mg entry** is implemented in the domain but not offered by default (`forBase(withMilligrams: false)`), because the 0.1 g store makes it coarse (250 mg → 0.3 g). Which screens, if any, offer mg is an owner decision.
- **Upper bound** is inherited from `Quantity.of` (100 000 g or ml), so 100 kg or 100 l is the largest entry.
- **Imperial**: not offered; OQ-02 remains with the owner.

## Stage 2 — entry in the sheets (implemented 2026-11-06)
- `portion_sheet.dart` (food logging): in custom-amount mode a `SegmentedButton<EntryUnit>` (key `portion-entry-unit`) offers the units of the food's basis (g | kg, or ml | l); the amount field's suffix and accepted decimals follow the chosen unit; the stored canonical amount is shown as "= 250 g" (key `portion-canonical`) whenever the unit is not canonical; switching unit converts the typed text (250 g ↔ 0.25 kg). `LogFood` receives the canonical amount exactly as before; the nutrient preview is unchanged.
- `ingredient_sheet.dart` (recipe ingredients): the same pattern (keys `ingredient-entry-unit`, `ingredient-canonical`); the saved `RecipeIngredient.quantity` is canonical g/ml, so recipe maths (spec §9) is untouched.
- `custom_food_screen.dart`: **not changed** — the serving-definition amounts stay in the food's reference unit. Adding a per-row unit there is small but not required by NUT-022's acceptance text; left for the owner to call.
- Parsing stays lenient (`double.tryParse`) in both sheets, as before; validation and the §2.1 store step happen in `EntryUnit.toCanonical` → `Quantity.of`.
- Tests: `portion_sheet_test.dart` +3 ("entry units follow the food basis and never cross it", "typing 0.25 kg previews and logs 250 g" — asserts the logged entry's quantity is 250 g and the preview shows 500 kcal, "switching g to kg converts the text and back"); new `ingredient_sheet_test.dart` (2: unit list for a per-100 g food; 0.5 l saved as 500 ml). Executed locally on Flutter 3.47.9 with the nutrition, recipe and spec-example suites: 254 passed, `flutter analyze --fatal-infos` clean. CI: pending on push.
- Independent review (this session, highest-capability model, of the implementing agent's diff): one finding — the portion sheet had moved to the strict plain-decimal parser, which would refuse a trailing separator the field's formatter allows; corrected before commit. No other finding.
