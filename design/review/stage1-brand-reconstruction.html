1 · Logo

RF monogram

The R is the structure. The F shares the R's top line and bowl line and emerges from behind the R's shoulder, so the amber reads as part of the same object rather than a second letter placed beside it. The leg of the R and the stem of the F share one base.

Primary monogram · light R, amber F, for dark surfaces
Monogram on light · slate R, amber F
Monochrome dark
Monochrome light
Amber on slate
Slate on amber · for amber brand panels

Construction

Upright geometry on a 128-unit grid, sheared 12°, then every corner rounded with true arcs. Horizontal lines at 0, 26, 48, 74 and 128 are shared by both letters. Vector masters are generated from this definition, not traced from the board.

Proportions

  • Stroke 26 (20% of cap height) for both letters.
  • Bowl 0–74, counter 26–48 × 26–66, leg 60→128 at 31° after slant.
  • Monogram box 185 × 128 (aspect 1.45:1).
  • F top bar extends 44 past its stem, mid bar 28, so the F stays an F at small sizes.

Corners and mass

  • Outer corners R6, inner corners R3, counter R10, bowl shoulder R30.
  • Only two colours, no outlines, no gradients on the mark itself.
  • The F is drawn behind the R. Monochrome versions are the union.

Clear space and minimum size

  • Clear space on all sides = one bar height (26 units, 20% of mark height).
  • Minimum: monogram 20 px tall on screen, 6 mm in print. Lockup 28 px tall.
  • Below 20 px use the app icon, never the lockup.

7 · Approval

Decisions needed before Stage 2

1
Monogram geometry. Approve the slant (12°), weight (stroke 20% of height) and the F-behind-R construction, or request a heavier / lighter / more upright variant.
2
Slate labels on amber buttons. Approve dark labels on primary buttons (AA) in place of the board's white labels.
3
Amber Ink #B45F00 for amber text and links on light surfaces, with #FF8A00 kept for fills and icons.
4
Muted text #8E9DAE in dark mode, replacing the brief's #8291A2.
5
App icon background: subtle slate gradient (shown) or flat Forge Slate.
6
Bottom navigation labels: Home · Train · Fuel · Track · Goals (pillar-based, shown) or the board's Home · Plan · Log · Progress · You.

After approval, Stage 2 delivers the full token set (JSON + CSS + Compose), the complete type scale, spacing, radius and elevation, and the icon library. Stage 3 the component library, Stage 4 the remaining screens, Stage 5 the developer handover.
