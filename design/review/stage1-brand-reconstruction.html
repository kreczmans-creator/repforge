1 · Logo

RF monogram

The R is the structure: an open-counter italic R whose leg sweeps under the F. The amber F is two pieces. Its top bar floats to the right of the R's bowl, and its mid bar carries a stem that leans on the R's leg. The R's bowl stroke completes the F, so the pair reads as one object.

Construction

Upright geometry on a 128-unit grid, sheared 25°, then every corner rounded with true arcs. Horizontal lines at 0, 30, 53, 82 and 128 are shared by both letters. Vector masters are generated from this definition; the supplied artwork is the reference, not the master.

Fit against the approved artwork

Supplied vector artwork at 55% opacity under the rebuilt mark at 55%. Where the two agree the colour is solid; fringes show the trace's edge noise and the small deviations of the clean geometry.

Proportions

  • Verticals 33, R stem 36, horizontals 30 (about 25% of cap height). Heavy by design.
  • Top bar 0–30, open counter 30–53, lower band 53–82, leg from the band to x 133 at 35° after slant.
  • Monogram box 256 × 128 (aspect 2:1). Bowl right edge 108; F top bar 112–196, F mid bar and stem 120–196.
  • Wordmark cap height 52% of the mark, centred on it, gap 35%.

Corners and mass

  • Outer corners R5, inner corners R3, counter R10, bowl shoulder R30.
  • Only two colours, no outlines, no gradients on the mark itself.
  • The F is drawn behind the R; the leg clips the F stem at the base. Monochrome versions are the union.

Clear space and minimum size

  • Clear space on all sides = one bar height (30 units, 23% of mark height).
  • Minimum: monogram 20 px tall on screen, 6 mm in print. Lockup 28 px tall.
  • Below 20 px use the app icon, never the lockup.

Rules

  • Grid 24, live area 22, stroke 2, round caps and joins. Never scale strokes; use the 20 / 24 / 32 px exports.
  • Solid fills only for small internal details (dots, cores, plates). No gradients on glyphs; the tile carries the gradient.
  • Home is an addition in the same language for the bottom navigation. Remaining brief icons (set, rep, timer, rest, PR, macros, water, barcode, measurements, photos, trends, history, milestone, search, notifications) are drawn in Stage 2 to these rules.

8 · Approval

Decisions needed before Stage 2

1
Monogram geometry. Approve the clean rebuild of the supplied artwork (overlay above) as the master, or ship the supplied traced SVGs as-is and accept their edge noise at large sizes.
1a
Wordmark typeface. Sora ExtraBold oblique is a close match to the supplied lettering, not identical. Approve Sora, or name the original face so the wordmark can be set from it.
1b
Flat or gradient. Flat masters for the app and icons, gradient variants only for hero, splash and marketing (as shown). Confirm, or make gradient the primary treatment.
2
Slate labels on amber buttons. Approve dark labels on primary buttons (AA) in place of the board's white labels.
3
Amber Ink #B45F00 for amber text and links on light surfaces, with #FF8A00 kept for fills and icons.
4
Muted text #8E9DAE in dark mode, replacing the brief's #8291A2.
5
App icon background: subtle slate gradient (shown) or flat Forge Slate.
6
Bottom navigation labels: Home · Train · Fuel · Track · Goals (pillar-based, shown) or the board's Home · Plan · Log · Progress · You.
7
Iconography. Approve the vector reconstruction of the reference sheet as the icon language. Stage 2 extends it to the full brief list.

After approval, Stage 2 delivers the full token set (JSON + CSS + Compose), the complete type scale, spacing, radius and elevation, and the icon library. Stage 3 the component library, Stage 4 the remaining screens, Stage 5 the developer handover.
